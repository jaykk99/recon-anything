ReconAnything NN — plugin-finding triage scorer

Pure-stdlib MLP. Ranks findings by how much they look like past candidates that survived triage.
TRIAGE ONLY. This network never verifies a vulnerability and never predicts VERIFIED_REAL — there are zero verified-real positives in the training data. A high score means “worth a live probe”, nothing more. A low score means “looks like the false positives we have seen”. Detection only: scoring never sends transactions or takes funds.
