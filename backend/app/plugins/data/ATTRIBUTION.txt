10k-most-common.txt is the 10,000 most common passwords list from
SecLists (https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists),
Passwords/Common-Credentials/10k-most-common.txt, by Daniel Miessler
and contributors, used under its MIT license.

It is bundled server-side for ReconAnything's DIRECT-MODE hash tool
only: it is tried against hashes a user explicitly submits for
assessment. It is never used against hashes found by scanning.
