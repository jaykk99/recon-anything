# $100 Website Security Scan: offer kit

Goal: first $100 from ReconAnything by selling a passive scan report to a local business that owns its website.

## Rules (keep these)
- Only scan a site after the owner says yes in writing (text or email is fine).
- Passive mode only for the first report. No logins, no changes, nothing that could break the site.
- Mask any leaked secrets in the report.

## Who to pitch (20 a day)
Winnipeg / Steinbach / Grunthal businesses with a website and customer data: dental and physio clinics, restaurants with online ordering, trades and contractors with booking forms, small retailers, real-estate agents, accountants.

## Message 1: cold text / email (send this)
Subject: Free 2-minute check of {business} website

Hi {name}, it's Jay from Grunthal. I run security checks on small-business websites. I took a quick look at {site} from the outside and noticed a couple of things worth fixing (for example {one real finding, e.g. missing security headers / expired certificate soon / exposed admin page}).

I can send you a plain-English report with fixes for $100, and I'll walk you through it. With your OK I'd run the full check first. Want me to go ahead?

Jay

## Message 2: follow-up after 2 days
Hi {name}, just following up on the website check. Happy to send a free summary of the top issue so you can see what the report looks like. No pressure.

## Report template (what the $100 buys)
1. Summary: score out of 10, three biggest risks in plain words.
2. Findings table: issue, why it matters, how to fix, how hard (easy / medium / hard).
3. What's already good (builds trust).
4. 30-minute call to walk through fixes.
5. Offer: $25/month monitoring that re-scans monthly and alerts on changes.

## Pricing
- One-time report: $100
- Fix-it add-on: $75/hour or flat quote
- Monitoring: $25/month

## Daily target
20 messages, 3 replies, 1 sale = $100 on day one or two.
